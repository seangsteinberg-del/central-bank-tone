How to read this

  • Federal Reserve only. This is where free market ground truth exists; the other seven institutions are scored but not validated against rates here.
  • The 2-year yield is the market-path proxy. It reprices freely with the expected policy path, unlike the highly persistent effective fed funds rate (whose forward correlation partly reflects a hawkish regime simply staying hawkish).
  • Correlation, not PnL. These are in-sample correlations over a single hiking-and-cutting cycle with wide monthly standard errors; a research signal, not a trade.
  • Cached market data. The FRED series are cached snapshots (latest month shown above), not live ticks.
  • The headline is one model's holistic judgement; the rate-path and the cross-check uncertainty band on each speech are what surface when to distrust it.
